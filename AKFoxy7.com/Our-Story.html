AK & Foxy

"We were inspired by losing our favourite beer at our local pub, we started learning the brewing process and soon became obsessed with the idea of having our own home brew."

AKFoxy7 started with a simple idea: dive headfirst into the art, science, and absolute chaos of home brewing from the ground up. Do it the hard way, learn from our mistakes and drink a lot of beer.

"Please check out our Instagram it's almost good."

Brew days are us sat in a garage, being fully dedicated to documenting the entire journey, never letting it boil over and cause twice as much cleaning! We test out all our brews and fine-tune them. We plan to share every step, error, and breakthrough along the way.

Whether you're following us on Insta, supporting us, or sticking around to see what AKFoxy7 eventually grows into, thank you!
